# Jezaku Product Vision

> Strategic direction; capabilities described here are targets unless verified in implementation.

## Vision
Jezaku is an **extensible spatial experience platform** for creating, publishing, discovering, governing, and experiencing digital content and services in physical space through augmented reality and other spatial interfaces.

Jezaku adds a digital layer to the physical world. It does not alter, damage, obstruct, claim ownership of, or grant access to physical places or objects.

## Product layers
1. **Jezaku Core:** spatial domain, object/scene lifecycle, placement, discovery, interaction, device contracts, identity/access integration, and safety interfaces.
2. **Jezaku Platform:** optional assets, publishing, extensions, marketplace, merchant/tenant management, campaigns, analytics, moderation, and developer tooling.
3. **Jezaku Experiences:** frames, navigation, Qibla, tourism, education, commerce, public services, industrial assistance, events, and games.

Core must remain useful without marketplace, advertising, merchant, or other optional commercial services.

## Principles
- Domain-agnostic spatial primitives; vertical workflows belong in experiences or extensions.
- Smartphone-first, device- and AR-provider-agnostic contracts.
- Physical-world respect: safety, property, law, consent, and legitimate authority.
- Safety before engagement, discovery ranking, advertising, or rendering.
- Separate domain truth from presentation: destinations and permissions are not arrows or HUDs.
- Privacy by design for location, camera, sensors, identity, movement, and social data.
- Versioned, permission-declared, validated, sandboxed, revocable extensions.
- Offline-aware behavior and preservation of user-created work.
- Accessible alternatives to AR, clear instructions, and localization.
- Evidence-based claims: distinguish implemented behavior from proposals and simulated validation from real-device validation.

## Experience domains
Personal memories and frames; navigation and wayfinding; Qibla; merchant presence and opt-in proximity promotions; tourism and museums; education; public services; industrial assistance; events, games, social experiences, and persistent shared spaces; future smart-glasses and XR experiences.

## Ecosystem roles
Viewer, creator, owner/publisher, tenant/organization, merchant, developer, moderator/operator, and authenticated authority/property controller. Ownership, publishing authority, and viewing permission are distinct.

## Success measures
Object creation/placement/restoration reliability; tracking quality; discovery relevance and policy correctness; safe navigation and fallbacks; extension compatibility and revocation; creator and marketplace quality; privacy/safety incidents; offline completion; accessibility; and verified outcomes. Distinguish impressions, interactions, estimated visits, and verified redemptions.

## Constraints and non-goals
Jezaku does not guarantee a physical location is safe. A geographic coordinate alone does not guarantee visual anchoring. Marketplace approval does not make executable code trusted. Public digital content does not grant physical access or placement rights. Smart-glasses capabilities vary. Existing frame sharing, audience, moderation, and on-site radius rules must not be weakened.
